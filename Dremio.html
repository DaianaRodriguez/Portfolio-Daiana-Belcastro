Analytics & Conversion

Optimizing Dremio’s Website
Through Analytics & AI

  • Client
    Dremio
  • Year
    2025/2026
  • Role
    Senior Product Designer

Introduction

Dremio is a data lakehouse platform that helps organizations bring together, query, and analyze data across different sources, supporting modern analytics and AI workloads.

Dremio’s main website is a critical touchpoint for prospects evaluating the platform, exploring pricing, and deciding whether to engage further with the company. To improve the website experience, we acquired Contentsquare to better understand how users were navigating the site, where they were dropping off, and which areas were creating friction throughout the decision-making journey.

The goal was to turn behavioral data into actionable design improvements that could support both user experience and business performance.

This project focused on three key areas:
  • Homepage conversion and engagement
  • Pricing page journey optimization
  • Website performance and Core Web Vitals improvements

Analytics-Led Design Approach

After introducing Contentsquare, I analyzed user behavior across key website journeys using heatmaps, session recordings, scroll behavior, click patterns, and drop-off data.

This helped uncover where users were engaging but not progressing, where important CTAs lacked visibility, and where friction was affecting conversion-critical journeys.

To move faster, I relied heavily on Contentsquare’s AI assistant to query the data in plain language, surface patterns and get to the key insights quickly, instead of building every report and analysis by hand.

I translated these behavioral insights into design decisions around page structure, content hierarchy, CTA placement, navigation flow, and performance priorities, using data to guide both design and optimization decisions.




1. Optimizing the Homepage

The homepage evolved from a more static marketing page into a more product-oriented and conversion-focused experience.

The Challenge

Analytics showed that the homepage had strong traffic, but users were not consistently moving deeper into the product journey.

Key issues included:
  • Low conversion rate and high bounce rate
  • Engagement dropping significantly below the fold
  • Low scroll depth, with users rarely reaching lower sections
  • Most clicks concentrated in the hero area and top navigation
  • Minimal interaction with lower-page sections such as features, FAQs, and footer

The page was too marketing-heavy and required users to navigate through too much content before understanding the product.


Redesigning the Experience

The new direction focused on making the homepage clearer, faster, and more product-led.

We brought product value propositions higher in the experience, simplified the storytelling, strengthened CTAs, and created clearer paths into product pages. Product and demo experiences were also embedded directly into the page, while a clearer visual diagram helped users understand the platform faster. The goal was to reduce friction and help prospects understand Dremio and continue exploring with less effort.

An AI-assisted design workflow

AI was part of the process from the first idea to the hand-off:

  • Mock-ups in Claude Design: I explored layouts and created the homepage mock-ups in Claude Design, including the copy, so ideas could be reviewed as realistic pages from the start.
  • Faster content reviews: I shared the Claude Design version with the content team, who edited the copy directly in the design. The approved version then went straight to the developers, cutting out rounds of back-and-forth.
  • Interactive product demo: I built the interactive demo myself with Claude, feeding it the product information so prospects could experience Dremio directly on the homepage.
As a result:
  • Conversion: Increased from 0.84% to 2.45%, a 192% improvement
  • Bounce rate: Decreased from 72.39% to 69.57%
  • Exit rate: Decreased from 61.53% to 58.90%
  • Engagement: Increased from 31.09% to 42.53%

Users also showed stronger exploration across product experiences, indicating that the new structure was guiding them more effectively through the website journey.

2. Reframing the Pricing Experience

The Pricing page was one of the most important pages in the evaluation journey, but analytics showed that it was behaving more like a dead-end than a conversion hub.

The Challenge

Previously, 73% of users immediately left after visiting Pricing. Users often returned to Home or Product pages, looped between Pricing and Products, or explored documentation without a clear next step.

This suggested that the page was not providing enough clarity, confidence, or direction to move users forward.



Turning Pricing Into a Conversion Hub

The redesign focused on supporting comparison, building trust, and creating clearer next steps.

We moved product information higher on the page, introduced a stronger Book a Demo CTA, added trust-driven proof points, and created a comparison table with a highlighted recommended option. A Try for Free CTA was also introduced further down the page, alongside clearer paths into Product, documentation, and use-case content.

The goal was to shift Pricing from a stopping point into an active research and conversion experience.

AI in the pricing process
  • Competitive research with Perplexity: I used Perplexity to research and compare how competitors structure their pricing pages, plans and CTAs, which helped shape the comparison table and the recommended-option approach.
  • Rapid mock-ups with Figma Make: I used Figma Make to quickly generate and try different layout options before refining the final design.

Trying different AI tools is part of how I work every day. Choosing the right one for each task helps me move from research to design faster.


The results showed a clear behavioral shift:
  • Immediate drop-off improved from 73% to 51%
  • More users continued into documentation and use cases
  • Engagement increased as users stayed longer in the evaluation flow
  • Users explored Product pages more frequently

The Pricing page was no longer simply a destination to check prices. It became a more active part of the product discovery journey.



A/B Testing and Continued Optimization

We are currently running an A/B test on the Pricing details page to understand which CTA placement better supports engagement and conversion.

The test is helping us validate design decisions through real user behavior rather than assumptions, while providing a foundation for continued optimization.

Improving Website Performance

Alongside the UX improvements, analytics and technical audits revealed that website performance was also affecting the experience, particularly on mobile.

The April performance review highlighted issues including heavy JavaScript and third-party tags, render-blocking and unused CSS, large media and animation payloads, and conflicts affecting Marketo forms.

These were not only technical problems. They directly affected how quickly users could understand the product, interact with key pages, submit forms, and move through conversion-critical journeys.




Working With Engineering

I partnered with Engineering to prioritize improvements across the website theme and platform configuration.

The work focused on improving speed while protecting essential functionality, including forms, navigation, tracking, conversion paths, and product exploration.

From the April baseline to the latest May test batch:
  • Average desktop performance improved from ~60 to 95
  • Average mobile performance improved from ~40 to 64
  • Largest Contentful Paint improved from 4.89s to 1.64s
  • Several desktop pages reached performance scores in the high 90s

The homepage was no longer a major performance bottleneck, creating a faster and more stable experience across important entry points.

To keep it that way, the developer built an automated tool that checks the performance levels of our key pages periodically. Instead of discovering problems months later, we now catch regressions as soon as they appear and can fix them before they affect users.




Looking Ahead

The next phase focuses on mobile stability and heavier page behavior. Priorities include improving mobile score consistency, tuning heavy JavaScript and vendor tags, and continuing to optimize the most demanding pages without affecting forms, tracking, or conversion flows.

Optimization is an ongoing process. Rather than treating the redesign as a finished product, the goal is to continuously observe behavior, identify friction, test improvements, and refine the experience.

Conclusion

This project demonstrated how behavioral analytics can become a powerful design tool when insights are translated into focused action.

By combining Contentsquare data, AI tools such as Contentsquare’s AI assistant and Claude, UX design and technical collaboration, I helped improve three critical areas of Dremio’s website: the homepage, the Pricing experience, and overall performance.

The result was a more product-led, conversion-focused, and performance-conscious website that helped users understand Dremio faster, explore the product more effectively, and move through the evaluation journey with less friction.
