About me

About me

Hello stranger

Nice to meet you

I'm Daiana, an AI-driven Senior Product Designer with 10+ years of experience creating meaningful digital experiences for brands like Dremio, Dell Technologies and Burger King.

Currently at Dremio, a data lakehouse platform, I use behavioural analytics to drive the design of the company's website. By redesigning the homepage and pricing journey, and partnering with engineering on performance, we nearly tripled homepage conversion and turned the pricing page from a dead end into a key step in the buying journey.

Before that, at Dell Technologies, I led product design for the Dell Design System website, working with a team of four designers to drive innovation, guide design decisions and create experiences grounded in user research.

AI is at the heart of how I work. I use Contentsquare's AI analytics to uncover how people really behave, Claude Design and Figma Make to explore ideas and prototype quickly, and Weavy to create visual assets. This frees me to focus on what really matters: understanding people and solving the right problems. Paired with a strong foundation in human-centred design, including six UX courses with the Nielsen Norman Group, it helps me turn complex problems into intuitive, impactful experiences.

Fluent in Spanish, English, Italian and Portuguese, and currently learning Japanese, I've lived in Buenos Aires, Milan, Belo Horizonte and Lisbon. My love for languages and cultures keeps shaping how I see the world and how I design.
